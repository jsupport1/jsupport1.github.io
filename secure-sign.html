Frequently Asked Questions

Are my digital signatures legally binding?

Electronic signatures are legally recognized in many jurisdictions (like E-SIGN in the US). However, for high-stakes legal documents, always consult local laws.

How is my privacy protected?

We use local WebAssembly processing. Your document and your signature never leave your device. We do not store or see any of your personal information.

Can I add multiple signatures?

Yes, you can draw and place as many signatures or initials as needed on any page of the document.
