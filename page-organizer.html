Frequently Asked Questions

Does reordering affect page quality?

No. We perform a "low-level" copy of the PDF page objects, ensuring that all text, images, and formatting remain identical to the original.

Can I organize very large PDFs?

Yes. Our tool is optimized to handle large documents. Thumbnails are rendered at a resolution that balances visual clarity with browser performance.

Is there a limit to the number of pages?

There is no hard limit, though performance may vary depending on your device's memory. We recommend processing documents up to 500 pages for the best experience.
