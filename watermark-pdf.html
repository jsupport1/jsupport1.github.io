Frequently Asked Questions

Will watermarking increase my file size?

Text watermarks add negligible size to your PDF. Image watermarks will increase the size slightly depending on the resolution of the image you upload.

Can I remove a watermark later?

Once a watermark is "burned" into a PDF, it becomes part of the document's content layer. This makes it difficult to remove without professional PDF editing software.

Does it watermark every page?

Yes, our tool automatically applies your chosen watermark to every single page of the uploaded PDF in one operation.
